About Simba Supermarket

Company Profile

Simba Supermarket is Rwanda's largest and most trusted retail chain, serving thousands of customers daily with fresh produce, groceries, and household goods.

Beliefs & Values

We believe in quality, affordability, customer satisfaction, and community support.

Origin & History

Established in Kiyovu, Kigali, Simba started as a family business and has grown over decades into a premier retail hub across Rwanda.
